## Design Context

### Users
A single operator running the 예시 카페 (whisky/cognac club) Naver cafe. They glance at this app in passing throughout the day — checking whether the welcome-comment automation is healthy, clearing the approval queue, occasionally tuning policy or templates — not sitting with it for long sessions. It runs tray-resident; most visits are seconds long.

### Brand Personality
아늑하고 취향 있는 위스키 감성 — cozy, tasteful, whisky-collector sensibility. Confident and quietly discerning rather than flashy. The feeling of a well-kept bar shelf or a tasting note card, not a SaaS ops dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Reference: retail whisky brand label/packaging craft — the restrained typographic confidence of a bottle label (fine rules, generous letter-spacing on small caps/labels) paired with a warm, low-saturation amber-on-near-black (dark) / amber-on-cream (light) palette. Anti-reference: generic SaaS dashboard chrome (Linear/Vercel-style flat utility) — this should feel warmer and more crafted than that, even though the actual job is operational (status, queues, settings).

**Typography stays single-family.** A system-serif accent for brand moments (app title, page headings) was tried and rejected — it read as awkward, not crafted. Every piece of text, brand or data, stays in Pretendard. Label-craft comes from spacing, rules, and weight, not from a second typeface.

Both light and dark themes are first-class and always follow the OS (`prefers-color-scheme`) — no manual toggle, no mode hardcoded as primary.

### Design Principles
1. **One typeface, console-clear data.** Brand moments (app title, section headers, the cafe's own image) get emphasis through weight, size, and letter-spacing within Pretendard — not a second (serif) font family. Numbers, statuses, and controls stay in the same clean sans the operator scans at a glance.
2. **Glanceable over decorative.** Every screen is read in seconds, not studied — hierarchy and whitespace do the work; ornamentation never competes with status colors or counts.
3. **Stay inside the 3-colour budget.** Surface/ink/accent plus the documented ok/warn/alarm exception is the whole palette. Refinement means sharper use of spacing, weight, and rhythm within that budget, not new colours.
4. **Warm, never neon.** The amber accent reads like cask wood and label foil, not a startup gradient — keep saturation low, let oklch lightness carry emphasis.
5. **Both themes equally intentional.** Every design decision is checked in both the light and dark `prefers-color-scheme` branches before it's considered done.
